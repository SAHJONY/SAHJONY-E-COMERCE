# SAHJONY — PREMIUM BRANDS FOR LESS

Independent multi-brand ecommerce foundation for the SAHJONY buy-after-sale model.

## Operating model

1. Customer shops on SAHJONY.
2. Customer payment is confirmed by SAHJONY.
3. SAHJONY rechecks source availability, price and post-fulfillment margin.
4. If gates pass, SAHJONY buys the product from an approved resale source.
5. The product ships to SAHJONY.
6. SAHJONY receives and inspects SKU, condition and provenance evidence when applicable.
7. SAHJONY repacks the order and ships it to the customer.
8. Tracking, delivery, returns and financial contribution are owned by SAHJONY.

## Safety posture

Production procurement remains fail-closed until payment, database, sourcing, tax, returns and carrier integrations are configured and verified.

## Initial gates

- Minimum contribution margin: 25%
- Minimum contribution per order: $25
- Source stock must be available
- Economics include product cost, inbound shipping, SAHJONY handling, outbound shipping and estimated payment fees

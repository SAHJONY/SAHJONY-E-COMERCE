# SAHJONY Catalog Sourcing Priority Matrix

## Scoring
Score each source from 1–5 on authorization strength, margin potential, inventory depth, fulfillment simplicity, and brand fit. Activation requires source evidence regardless of score.

| Source | Authorization strength | Margin potential | Inventory depth | Fulfillment simplicity | Brand fit | Priority |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Perfumes Plus Wholesale | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 1 |
| Ready Distribution | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 1 |
| Shinola Wholesale | 5 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 1 |
| Stratum Co. | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 2 |

## Evidence basis
- Perfumes Plus states it is the exclusive distributor of ARMAF and Sterling Parfums across the Americas and operates a wholesale fragrance catalog with substantial SKU depth.
- Ready Distribution states its wholesale inventory is brand-authorized, requires retailer credentials and MAP compliance, and currently displays brands including Hoka, On Cloud, Brooks, Saucony, Merrell, OOFOS, Altra and Crocs.
- Shinola offers a direct authorized-retailer wholesale program for watches, leather goods and lifestyle products.
- Stratum states it provides authenticated inventory and transaction-level authorization documentation across multiple premium brands.

## Activation order
1. Fragrance: first 5–8 SKUs after account/invoice evidence.
2. Footwear: first 5–8 SKUs after retailer approval, exact SKU access and MAP review.
3. Shinola: first 2–4 watches/leather SKUs after brand-direct retailer approval.
4. Multi-brand closeouts: opportunistic only after exact lot documentation, image rights and fulfillment terms are known.

## Hold rules
- No exact SKU = HOLD.
- No commercial evidence = HOLD.
- No image rights = HOLD.
- No current inventory = HOLD.
- Unknown MAP/channel restrictions = HOLD.
- Unknown returns/fulfillment constraints = HOLD.

## Launch target
- 25 candidates evaluated.
- 10+ exact SKUs approved before Stripe.
- Minimum 2 independently verified sourcing relationships before broad launch.
- No single source should represent >60% of sellable SKUs by the 30-day target.
